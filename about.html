About GiftCardSale

Connecting global consumers to digital value through trusted partnerships

Who We Are

GiftCard Sale LLC is a US-registered digital marketplace specializing in discounted gift cards for gaming, streaming, shopping, and entertainment. Founded on the principle that digital commerce should be fast, secure, and accessible, we serve customers across Brazil, the United States, and international markets.

We are not a traditional retailer. Instead, we operate as a technology-driven distribution platform that bridges the gap between major digital content providers and end consumers.

Our Partnership-Driven Model

Our entire operation is built on strategic collaborations with verified distributors, regional suppliers, and payment technology providers. This partnership ecosystem allows us to:

Global Distributors

Direct integration with authorized wholesale networks to ensure code authenticity and instant availability.

Payment Partners

Secure transactions powered by Stripe, PayPal, Mercado Pago, and blockchain settlement networks.

Security Providers

SSL encryption, fraud detection AI, and PCI DSS compliance maintained by certified third-party auditors.

By leveraging these partnerships, we eliminate traditional retail markups and pass the savings directly to you, while maintaining enterprise-grade reliability.

How We Operate

  • Step 1: We source digital codes in bulk through authorized B2B channels and regional distributors.
  • Step 2: All codes undergo automated validation checks before being listed on our platform.
  • Step 3: When you complete a purchase, our API automatically retrieves a fresh code from our partner inventory.
  • Step 4: The code is delivered instantly to your registered email via encrypted delivery channels.

This fully automated, partner-integrated workflow ensures zero manual handling, faster delivery times, and lower operational costs.
